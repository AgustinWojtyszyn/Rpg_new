# Content guide

## Visual direction

Target: colorful premium cartoon action, readable on a phone screen. Programmer-art colors in the prototype are semantic only. Final PixelLab content should keep strong silhouettes, limited detail at gameplay scale, readable telegraphs, consistent top-down perspective, outline thickness and light direction.

## Enemy families

- Dinosaurs: pursuit, flanking, charges and momentum. Anchor: Raptor Scout; future Pachy, Dilophosaurus and T-Rex boss.
- Plants: area denial, grabbing and forced positioning. Anchor: Root Vine.
- Aliens: ranged pressure, teleportation and unusual projectile rules. Anchor: Orb Stalker.
- Skeletons: readable classic melee/ranged units used as pacing anchors. Anchor: Bone Guard.
- Insects: armor, swarms and movement pressure. Anchor: Iron Beetle.

## Boss rule

A phase transition may change behavior, spawn enemies, alter the arena or move the player to another arena. It must not grant generic boss immunity.

## T-Rex rule

Normal charges do not destroy the dungeon. One or two authored set pieces per fight may respond to a special charge, optionally prepared with a lever. The visual result can be dramatic while the geometry change stays deterministic and testable.
